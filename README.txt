THE CRAVE CRAFT — WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: single-page website
- styles.css: responsive styling and animations
- script.js: category filtering, menu search, mobile navigation, reveal effects
- menu-data.js: menu data
- assets/cravecraft-logo.jpg: uploaded brand logo

Preview locally:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser. (An internet connection is needed for the Google Fonts.)
3. To publish, drag the extracted folder into Netlify Drop or deploy it through your Netlify site.

Menu data note:
The page lists 92 items from the Pre Menu sheet, grouped into categories. Where a corresponding TCCM record was found, the page uses its fancy name, description, carbs, protein, and Swiggy/Zomato price. 26 pre-menu items did not have an unambiguous matching TCCM record, so they are shown without invented nutrition or price data and marked "Details coming soon". Please confirm those against the workbook before launch.

No production costs or ingredient lists are displayed.
